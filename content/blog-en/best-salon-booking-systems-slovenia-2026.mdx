The useful system is the one your team can use correctly for everyday work. Define three essential requirements and run the same test with each provider. This article is prepared by Jedro+, so it is a vendor’s review rather than an independent market ranking.

## Test the same booking

Create a service, select a staff member and book as a client in each system. Reschedule and cancel. Check the client view, calendar and messages. Include room or equipment availability if needed.

## Jedro+: check the plan and communication

[Free](/en/pricing) includes a basic calendar and client records; compare Plus and Pro for online booking and regular reminders. Plus costs €14.90 with monthly billing or €142.80 annually. SMS allowances, add-ons and [Receptionist+](/en/features/receptionist-plus) are separate considerations. Prices exclude VAT.

## Lime Booking: consult primary sources

The official [business page](https://lime-booking.com/sl/business) and [pricing](https://business.lime-booking.com/si/cenik/) describe bookings, notifications and several plans. Distinguish monthly billing from the monthly equivalent of an annual price. Read our [verified comparison](/en/compare/jedroplus-vs-lime-booking) for details.

## Calculate your actual usage cost

List users, locations, required messages and call minutes. Request a quote with every necessary add-on. Assess fiscal functionality for the country of use; the Jedro+ FURS module is for Slovenia.

## Decide after testing

Compare completed tasks, team usability and total cost. Resolve open questions before rollout. Our [getting started guide](/en/blog/welcome-to-the-jedro-plus-blog) suggests a setup order.
