You do not need every automation on day one. Prepare one real service, one schedule and a test client. Then check the whole journey from booking to message. This guide is prepared by the Jedro+ editorial team; it describes a suggested setup process, not a customer results study.

## 1. Define how appointments work

Choose an initial service, its duration, price and staff member. Add working hours and breaks. If it requires a particular room or device, review [resource management](/en/features/resources). Test a longer service near the end of the working day.

## 2. Check client records

Start with a small reviewed sample. Check names, phone numbers and email addresses. Do not merge two people simply because they share a phone number. Our [import preparation guide](/en/blog/import-clients-into-jedroplus) separates data preparation from the transfer itself.

## 3. Make a test booking

Open [online booking](/en/features/online-booking) as a client and reserve an appointment. Check the service, time and staff member in the calendar. Reschedule and cancel it, then check the updated calendar and notifications. Use your own contact details when testing messages.

## 4. Choose an initial scope

Free includes the basic calendar and client records. Compare [paid plans](/en/pricing) for regular reminders and online booking. Receptionist+ uses separately purchased minutes and provides 30 trial minutes on first activation. Test one scenario before extending usage.

## 5. Review the first week

Record where clients get stuck, whether service durations are correct and whether messages reach the right contact. Share the booking link publicly after testing the journey. Repeat this check when adding a service or changing the team.
