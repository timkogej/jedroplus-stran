An AI receptionist answers a phone call, introduces itself on behalf of the business and helps arrange the next step. [Receptionist+](/en/features/receptionist-plus) connects to the Jedro+ calendar: it books, reschedules or cancels appointments, answers questions and records the agreement. The conversation below is illustrative, not a recording of a real client.

## A booking example

1. Assistant: “Salon Luna, hello! I’m the salon’s digital assistant. How can I help you?”
2. The client wants a haircut. The assistant explains that several appointments are available and asks for a preferred day.
3. The client chooses Thursday. The assistant offers available times at 13:00, 14:00 and 15:00.
4. The client chooses 14:00. The assistant confirms the details and records the appointment in the calendar.

The conversation follows the client’s language. Check actual offered times against your services, staff and schedules.

## Prepare before activation

Set up services, durations, opening hours and the business greeting. Every business receives its own phone number. For an EU business, check number availability and country-specific phone settings with the team during setup. Email info@jedroplus.com for help.

## Test the first call

Try a new booking, reschedule, cancellation and service question. Compare each agreement with the calendar and call log. Test a question without a prepared answer and agree the desired handling with the team. Interactive illustrations are on the [Receptionist+ page](/en/features/receptionist-plus#primer-klica).

## Understand minute billing

First activation provides 30 free minutes. Packs offer 100 minutes for €15, 300 for €39 and 750 for €89, excluding VAT. Minutes are purchased separately from the Jedro+ subscription and do not expire. Usage is proportional: 30 seconds uses 0.5 minutes; 90 seconds uses 1.5 minutes. Check [current pricing](/en/pricing#receptionist-plus) and choose according to actual conversation duration.
